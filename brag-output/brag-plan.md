# VoiceGateway console teaser

## Nine-question planning rubric

1. **What is it?** VoiceGateway is a profiler for LiveKit voice agents, following one call from the phone line to the model.
2. **Strongest claim:** One call, one story. The console joins the layers a developer normally has to inspect separately.
3. **Visual hook:** The real Live wire graph and turn anatomy, on the console's white canvas, make latency visible.
4. **Actual UI:** Captured Overview gauge, every-turn distribution, and Live wire graph. No invented interfaces or metrics.
5. **Shortest satisfying film:** 18 seconds. Enough to read a question, inspect three product moments, and remember the name.
6. **Tone:** Polished. A developer's microscope, with brisk cuts and quiet holds. The console provides the texture; no generic neon scenery.
7. **Audio:** Intentionally silent. The primary delivery is a README GIF, so every beat must work without sound. No voice, music, or SFX.
8. **Share caption:** Why was that call slow? A first look at VoiceGateway's console: follow a LiveKit call from the phone line to the model. OSS + Cloud V1 in progress.
9. **User flow:** Read the answer-time signal → inspect the turn distribution → follow the connected call path.

## The angle

“Why was that call slow?” is the developer's question. The film answers with the actual console, moving from the answer-time signal to the turn distribution and the connected system. It is a console preview, not a claim of a shipped backend. Captured figures are fixture data from the existing console.

## Visual identity

- Format: landscape, 1600 × 1000; README GIF 960 × 600.
- Duration: 18 seconds.
- Canvas: #ffffff and #f5f5f7.
- Ink: #1d1d1f; secondary ink: #6e6e73.
- Signal: #0071e3; teal #1f96aa reserved for the real wordmark.
- Type: system sans, matching the console. Outlined Inter lettering in the supplied brand SVG.
- One recurring header: real VoiceGateway wordmark and “Console preview”.
- No invented metrics, simulated backend activity, or claim that Cloud is launched.

## Poster

Choose the settled Live wire frame, because it presents the product and its purpose without needing playback. Bake that poster into MP4 frame zero; keep the GIF's opening question readable.

## Motion contract

The moving subject in the middle scenes is the screenshot wrapper. Each remains one continuous element: framing at entry, focused framing, final hold. Transitions replace one screen with another. Headline entries take no more than 0.5 seconds. Reframes settle before the reader inspects the UI.
